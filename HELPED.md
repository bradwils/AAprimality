# Assignment 3 — Change Log

Log of notable changes to this repo, per [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md) §3. A change is notable if it fixes more than two bugs, is a major rewrite or restructure, or is more than a small chore.

| Chose `uint64_t` as the integer type for Miller-Rabin | `implementations/miller_rabin (brad)/algorithm.cpp` | Covers `n` up to 2^64 − 1 at native speed. `mul_mod` needs a wider intermediate (`unsigned __int128`) because `a * b` can overflow. Larger inputs would need GMP. |
